# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers must make this project a respectful place to work. Be direct about technical disagreements and considerate toward the people involved.

Acceptable conduct includes:

- giving specific, actionable feedback;
- asking questions without belittling the person who asked;
- crediting other people's work;
- respecting privacy and stated boundaries;
- correcting mistakes openly.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, or publishing another person's private information.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact options on the [repository owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/ddoug220). Maintainers will review reports in confidence and may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or ban a participant when needed.

Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is not allowed.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, release discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the project.
